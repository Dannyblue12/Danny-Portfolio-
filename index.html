About Me

Design that looks right.
Code that holds up.

Hello! I'm Daniel, a passionate developer dedicated to creating seamless digital experiences that are both functional and beautiful. When I wrote my first code in QBASIC 9 years ago, it completely changed how I saw programming.

Since then, I've grown into a web developer with a strong eye for design, skilled in integrating APIs and using Express.js for backend solutions with MongoDB for handling databases. Outside of coding, I enjoy music, quality time, and sketching. My goal is to not just meet expectations, but to exceed them, delivering solutions that drive growth and make a lasting impact.
